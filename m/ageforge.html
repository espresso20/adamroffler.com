What is AgeForge?

AgeForge is an idle civilization game written in Go and shipped as a single binary for macOS, Linux and Windows. You steer by typing commands at a prompt; your civilization keeps working between commands, and when you open a save again it credits the time you were away.

A run climbs 22 ages in 7 epochs. Workers staff themselves, a build plan queues up to 60 builds, upgrades and trades, and the Map draws your town as a glyph world from the buildings you actually own. From the Iron Era on, a harbinger warns of the catastrophe ahead and you choose to Endure or Succumb. Prestige and Era Mastery make every later run faster.

Game Systems

302 buildings: 251 in 14 production lineages, plus storage, monuments and one wonder per age. You need an age's wonder to advance.

203 technologies in prerequisite chains across 24 resources, from food and wood up to Quantum Flux. Scholars come with your knowledge buildings.

Soldiers come with your military buildings, soften raids and march on short, long and grand missions, each a gamble of cost against reward.

The 7 Epochs

Primitive, Stone & Bronze Ages. Humanity's first steps. Wood, stone, and fire.

Iron, Classical & Medieval Ages. Empires of iron and faith rise and fall.

Renaissance, Colonial & Industrial Ages. Steam, steel, and global ambition.

Start Your Empire

AgeForge is free. Grab the binary for your platform from the latest GitHub release and run it. On Apple Silicon:

curl -fLo ageforge https://github.com/espresso20/ageforge/releases/latest/download/ageforge-macos-arm64 && chmod +x ageforge && ./ageforge Release downloads are version 3.6. Version 4.0, which the Compendium describes, is on the master branch and builds from source.
